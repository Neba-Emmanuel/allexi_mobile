Support

A little help, whenever you need it.

Questions about reading, a passage, or administrator access? We’re here to help.

Reading & editions

Allexi Bible is the default reading edition. Some chapters are still being prepared. Open the edition picker in the reader to see the available editions.

Reading & study

Read, bookmark, highlight and keep notes without signing in. The installed iOS and Android apps have no login. Published scripture updates are available when the app connects to the service.

Administrator access

Approved administrators sign in through Settings in the browser web app to update chapters and verses. Public account signup is not available. If you are an administrator and need a new password, choose Forgot password on the web and follow the recovery email instructions.

Deleting your account

Administrators can sign in to the browser web app, open Settings and choose Delete my account. Confirm with your current password. This removes the account and synced study data, and clears that account’s study data from the browser making the request. If you cannot sign in, email support with the subject Account deletion from your account email. We may need to verify account ownership. Readers do not need an account.

Reporting a passage or a problem

For a passage, include the edition, book, chapter and verse. For an app problem, include your device model, app version and what happened. Please do not send passwords, recovery codes or private study notes.
