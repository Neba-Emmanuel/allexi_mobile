Privacy policy

Your reading. Your privacy.

This policy describes how Allexi Bible handles information when you read, study or use administrator access on the web.

Last updated 26 November 2026

Reading without an account

You can read without creating an account. Your reading preferences and study annotations are kept in the app. The app does not include advertising, analytics tracking, or location or microphone collection.

Account information

Login is available only to approved administrators in the browser web app. Your email address and password are processed by Supabase Auth to provide sign-in and recovery. Browser sign-in sessions use browser storage. The installed iOS and Android apps do not offer login. Earlier app versions may have retained native sign-in sessions in the operating system’s secure storage; mobile study sync is no longer enabled.

What sync shares

For signed-in web administrators, notes, bookmarks, highlights and reading position are sent to our application service and stored in Supabase for sync. Account access controls restrict access to these study records. Editorial changes and revision history are stored to manage and publish scripture updates. Readers can receive published scripture without signing in.

Service providers & technical information

Supabase provides authentication and database services. Our hosting and email providers process information needed to run the service, including account recovery messages. Application logs contain request identifiers, route paths, status codes and durations. Hosting providers may also process network addresses and access logs. The application does not log passwords, access tokens or study content.

Retention & account deletion

Your account and synced study data are retained while your account is active. You can permanently delete them in the web app’s Settings after confirming your current password, which is used to verify the request and is not stored by our application service. Deletion also clears that account’s study data from the requesting device. Other devices may retain local copies until removed there. Published scripture and editorial revision history remain, with the deleted editor’s account link removed. Provider backups may persist under the provider’s retention arrangements; deletion does not immediately erase every backup copy.

Your choices & questions

Reading and studying do not require an account. Login and editorial tools are reserved for approved web administrators. For privacy questions, access or correction requests, or help deleting an account you cannot access, contact the support email below. Account-related requests may require ownership verification. Never send your password or recovery code.

Policy updates

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The date shown here identifies the latest revision. Contact us if you have a question about a change.
