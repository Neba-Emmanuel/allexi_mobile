Support

A little help, whenever you need it.

Questions about reading, your account, or a passage? We’re here to help.

Reading & editions

Allexi Bible is the default reading edition. Some chapters are still being prepared. Open the edition picker in the reader to see the available editions.

Syncing your study

Reading does not require an account. To keep notes, bookmarks, highlights and your reading place together across devices, sign in to the same account, connect to the internet and choose Sync now in Settings.

Recovering your account

Choose Forgot password in Settings and enter your account email. Use the recovery code in your email to set a new password. Check your spam folder if the message has not arrived.

Deleting your account

Sign in, open Settings and choose Delete my account. Confirm with your current password. This permanently removes your account and synced study data, and clears that account’s study data from the device making the request. If you cannot sign in, email support with the subject Account deletion from your account email. We may need to verify that the account belongs to you.

Reporting a passage or a problem

For a passage, include the edition, book, chapter and verse. For an app problem, include your device model, app version and what happened. Please do not send passwords, recovery codes or private study notes.
